3. Blockchain-Based Identity Verification
The current identity verification process relies heavily on centralized authorities, posing risks
of data breaches, identity theft, and privacy infringement. Additionally, the lack of
interoperability between identity systems complicates cross-border transactions and access
to services. Thus, there definitely is a pressing need for a decentralized identity verification
system that utilizes blockchain technology to securely authenticate individuals' identities
while preserving privacy and reducing reliance on centralized identity providers.
Scope of Work:
Here’s a basic idea of what you will be designing to solve this problem statement. Feel free
to ideate and have add-on features.
1. Security and privacy preservation being the main objective of this, there is a need to
ensure integrity and confidentiality of users’ data, through cryptographic techniques and
algorithms.
2. A smart contract for identity registration, verification and management.
3. Individuals register on the blockchain network by creating a digital identity which may
involve providing personal information such as name, date of birth, and
government-issued identification documents (tip: you may use college IDs – SRN/PRNs).
4. In a real-world scenario, identity verification could be done using biometric information.
Feel free to implement any verification method you may find apt in your case.
5. Creation of unique identifiers/digital signatures for the users’ data and decentralized
storage of user data. This identifier is used to authorize the user at any point.
6. Design of a user-friendly interface.

Ideate, design, find appropriate algorithms and come up with a prototype for this problem
statement.
Think about the organizations, firms and individuals who will use this application. Here are a
few to start with: Banks, government services, e-commerce.
